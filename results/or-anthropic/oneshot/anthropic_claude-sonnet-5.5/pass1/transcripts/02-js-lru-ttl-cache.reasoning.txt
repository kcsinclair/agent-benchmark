I'm designing the cache structure using a map that tracks insertion order for recency, with entries storing value and expiry. For touch operations I remove and reinsert; for size and keys I'm purging expired entries first before returning results, and handling the edge case of setting an already-expired TTL.

I'm weighing eviction strategy: whether to always purge expired entries before evicting LRU on overflow, and worrying this scan could be O(n) at full capacity rather than O(1), considering if a heap-based expiry tracker would be worth the added complexity.

I decide to use a min-heap keyed on expiry time with lazy invalidation — entries become stale when replaced or deleted, and the heap rebuilds once stale entries pile up too much, keeping purge operations amortized O(log n).
