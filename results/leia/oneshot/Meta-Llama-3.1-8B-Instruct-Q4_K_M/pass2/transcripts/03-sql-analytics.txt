Here are the four SQL files as requested:

### q1.sql — Revenue per customer

```sql
SELECT 
  c.name AS customer_name,
  ROUND(SUM(oi.quantity * p.unit_price), 2) AS revenue
FROM 
  customers c
  JOIN orders o ON c.id = o.customer_id
  JOIN order_items oi ON o.id = oi.order_id
  JOIN products p ON oi.product_id = p.id
WHERE 
  o.status = 'completed'
GROUP BY 
  c.name
ORDER BY 
  revenue DESC, 
  customer_name ASC;
```

### q2.sql — Top 3 products by units sold

```sql
SELECT 
  p.name AS product_name,
  SUM(oi.quantity) AS units_sold
FROM 
  products p
  JOIN order_items oi ON p.id = oi.product_id
  JOIN orders o ON oi.order_id = o.id
WHERE 
  o.status = 'completed'
GROUP BY 
  p.name
ORDER BY 
  units_sold DESC, 
  product_name ASC
LIMIT 3;
```

### q3.sql — Customers with no orders at all

```sql
SELECT 
  c.name AS customer_name,
  c.country
FROM 
  customers c
WHERE 
  c.id NOT IN (SELECT customer_id FROM orders);
```

### q4.sql — Monthly revenue for 2025

```sql
SELECT 
  strftime('%Y-%m', o.ordered_at) AS month,
  ROUND(SUM(oi.quantity * p.unit_price), 2) AS revenue
FROM 
  orders o
  JOIN order_items oi ON o.id = oi.order_id
  JOIN products p ON oi.product_id = p.id
WHERE 
  o.status = 'completed' AND 
  STRFTIME('%Y', o.ordered_at) = '2025'
GROUP BY 
  strftime('%Y-%m', o.ordered_at)
ORDER BY 
  month ASC;
```
